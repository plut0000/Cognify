What's the difference between mitosis and meiosis?
Mitosis makes 2 identical copies of a cell (used for growth). Meiosis makes 4 unique cells with half the DNA (used for reproduction). Easy way to remember: Mitosis = More of the same, Meiosis = Mix it up.
